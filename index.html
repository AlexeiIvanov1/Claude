How to Play

Objective

Defend the settlement from wasteland creatures by deploying defensive turrets along their path.

Turret Types

  • MG Turret (100 Caps): Rapid-fire ballistic weapon
  • Laser Turret (150 Caps): High-energy beam weapon, precision targeting
  • Missile Launcher (200 Caps): Explosive ordnance, devastates groups
  • Cryo Turret (125 Caps): Freezing projectiles slow targets

Wasteland Threats

  • Raider: Basic threat, standard speed and health
  • Feral Ghoul: Fast and aggressive but weak
  • Super Mutant: Heavily armored, slow but deadly
  • Bloodbug: Flying insect, immune to explosives
  • Deathclaw: Appears every 5 assaults - extremely dangerous!

Controls

  • Click turret button, then click grid to deploy
  • Click existing turret to upgrade or scrap
  • Press SPACE to begin next assault
  • Press ESC to pause
  • Use speed buttons to control V.A.T.S. time flow

Tips

  • Upgrade existing turrets before deploying new ones
  • Use missile launchers at corners where enemies cluster
  • Cryo turrets work great with high-damage weapons
  • Save caps for Deathclaw assaults!
