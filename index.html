How to Play

Objective

Prevent enemies from reaching your base by building towers along the path.

Tower Types

  • Basic Tower ($100): Fast fire rate, balanced damage
  • Sniper Tower ($150): Long range, high damage, slow fire
  • Splash Tower ($200): Area damage, great against groups
  • Slow Tower ($125): Slows enemies, moderate damage

Enemy Types

  • Basic: Normal speed and health
  • Fast: Quick but weak
  • Tank: Very slow but high health
  • Flying: Fast, immune to splash damage
  • Boss: Appears every 5 waves - very tough!

Controls

  • Click tower button, then click grid to place
  • Click existing tower to upgrade or sell
  • Press SPACE to start next wave
  • Press ESC to pause
  • Use speed buttons to control game speed

Tips

  • Upgrade existing towers before building new ones
  • Use splash towers at corners where enemies bunch up
  • Slow towers work great with high-damage towers
  • Save money for boss waves!
